# Latent dictionaries: what the edge-site latents do

> This file is generated by `tools/clrs_docs_generate.py` from the repository and the training outputs; do not edit it by hand. Re-run the generator after any change to the data.


These tables are the measured behaviour of the latents of the edge-site transcoders, one section per algorithm. The method is in `07-data-files-and-formats.md` (file `edge_tc/<algo>.latents.json`) and `11-testing-and-verification.md`.

How to read them:

- **fires on** is the share of all pair rows (sender -> receiver pairs at one step) on which the latent is among the 8 active ones.
- **effect** is the change of the receiver's main target logit per unit of the latent's activation, as mean +- standard deviation over all measured activations, and the share of activations where it is positive. For categorical targets the logit is that of the class the model predicts.
- **real edge / non-edge / self** is the share of the latent's firings that happen on pairs of that kind. Compare with the share of such pairs among all pairs (shown with each algorithm).
- **P / R / F1** are precision, recall and F1 of the best condition, scored on the held-out (odd-seed) graphs. The condition is chosen on the even-seed graphs. A latent is named only when its held-out F1 is at least 0.5 (tier B) or 0.8 (tier A).
- **examples** are (seed, step, sender, receiver, activation) for the three strongest activations; the page loads them with the "show" button.
